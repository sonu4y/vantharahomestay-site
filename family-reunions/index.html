Family Reunions & Get-Togethers

A Private 5BHK Villa for Family Reunions in Turkayamjal, Hyderabad

Three generations, one private villa — 5 bedrooms across 3 floors in Turkayamjal, Hyderabad.

VanThara Homestay is a fully private, 5-bedroom villa in Turkayamjal, Hyderabad, built for exactly this kind of gathering — three generations under one roof, without compromise. Instead of splitting your family across hotel rooms, book the entire villa and let everyone stay, cook, and celebrate together.

Why Families Choose VanThara for Reunions

A family reunion works best when nobody has to leave to find space. VanThara spreads 5 bedrooms across 3 floors, so couples, grandparents, and cousins each get their own corner while sharing a common living room, dining area, and rooftop terrace for the moments that matter.

Entire Villa, Fully Private

There are no shared common areas with other guests and no other bookings running alongside yours — the villa, gate, and compound belong to your family for the length of your stay.

Kitchen Built for Big Family Cooking

The fully equipped kitchen includes a Bosch dishwasher, microwave, and a large French-door refrigerator — enough capacity for family members to cook festival meals or everyday food together instead of ordering in.

Rooftop Terrace for Evening Get-Togethers

Once the day's plans wind down, the open rooftop terrace is where most families end up — for chai, late conversations, or watching the kids run around under open sky.

Space for Everyone — 5 Bedrooms Across 3 Floors

The Master Suite on Floor 1 has a king bed and attached bath; the Guest Bedroom on Floor 2 has a teak wood bed and calm, window-lit ambiance; and the Upper Floor Rooms on Floor 3 include a private lounge and direct gym access, so different generations can each settle where they're comfortable.

Things to Do Together at the Villa

Between meals, the home theatre (75-inch 4K with surround sound) is set up for family movie nights, the private gym gives early risers a place to work out, and the indoor water fountain and Ganesha idol in the lobby add a calm, homely welcome for elders.

Nearby Attractions for Family Outings

VanThara's location in Turkayamjal puts your family within reach of Ramoji Film City, one of Hyderabad's best-known attractions for multi-generational visits, with the Outer Ring Road (ORR) providing straightforward access to the rest of the city and LB Nagar.

Ramoji Film City

A popular full-day outing for families staying in the area, combining film-set tours with gardens and entertainment zones suited to all ages.

Outer Ring Road (ORR) Connectivity

The villa's position near the ORR keeps travel time reasonable for family members arriving from different parts of Hyderabad or from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport.
