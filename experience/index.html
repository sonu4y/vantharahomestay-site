Available On Request

No — it's a premium concierge add-on available on request and subject to confirmation and an additional charge, not a standard inclusion.

Can barbecue be arranged during a stay?

Yes — a barbecue setup on the terrace or garden can be arranged on request; message us ahead of your stay to organize it.

How far is VanThara from Ramoji Film City and Wonderla Hyderabad?

VanThara is in Turkayamjal, among the closer major residential areas to Ramoji Film City, with Wonderla also reachable for a day trip. Exact drive time depends on traffic and your route, so we recommend checking live directions before you head out.
