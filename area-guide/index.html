A Local Guide to Turkayamjal & South East Hyderabad

Everything to know about the area around VanThara Homestay — landmarks, access, and what's nearby.

Turkayamjal doesn't get the same spotlight as Hyderabad's more well-known neighborhoods, but it's a well-connected part of the city's South East corridor — home to major landmarks like Ramoji Film City and a growing IT and aerospace employment base. Here's what to know about the area around VanThara Homestay.

Where Turkayamjal Sits in Hyderabad

Turkayamjal is a locality in the Rangareddy district, part of Hyderabad's South East postal division (PIN 501510), within the LB Nagar administrative zone. It sits near the Outer Ring Road (ORR), which connects it efficiently to the rest of the city without routing through central Hyderabad traffic.

Landmarks Near VanThara Homestay

A few reference points guests and drivers consistently use to find their way to the villa:

Ramoji Film City

One of the most-visited attractions in the region, combining film sets, gardens, and entertainment zones — a popular pairing for guests extending a villa stay into a day trip.

TCS Adibatla

A major IT campus in the area, drawing corporate travelers and teams who use VanThara Homestay as an offsite base close to work.

Tata Aerospace SEZ

Part of Hyderabad's growing aerospace and manufacturing corridor, within reach of the villa for business travelers and teams.

Outer Ring Road (ORR)

The ORR is the main connector between Turkayamjal and the rest of Hyderabad, keeping travel times reasonable despite the area's outskirts location.

Nearby Localities Worth Knowing

The wider LB Nagar zone includes Vanasthalipuram, Hayathnagar, Nadargul, Dilsukhnagar, Nagole, and Uppal — all within the same general corridor as Turkayamjal, useful reference points if you're coordinating a pickup or giving directions to guests unfamiliar with the area.

Getting to Turkayamjal

From Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, the route runs via the Outer Ring Road. From central Hyderabad, LB Nagar is the main junction to route through. Message us on WhatsApp before your stay and we'll share specific directions based on where you're traveling from.
