From the gold-accented entrance gate to the chandelier-lit staircase, VanThara Homestay was designed with the kind of detail that photographs well — which is exactly why families have chosen it for pre-wedding shoots and saree functions in Turkayamjal, Hyderabad.

A Photogenic Villa Built for Celebrations

Every floor of the villa offers a different visual mood — ornate and golden at the entrance, warm and chandelier-lit in the living room, open and airy on the rooftop — giving photographers several distinct setups without needing to change locations.

Gold-Accented Entrance Gate

The villa's entrance gate, with its gold detailing, is one of the most-photographed spots on the property and a strong opening shot for a pre-wedding sequence.

Grand Curved Staircase & Chandelier-Lit Living Room

A sweeping curved staircase beside a five-arm vintage brass chandelier gives couples and families a formal, editorial backdrop inside the villa.

Rooftop Terrace for Golden-Hour Shots

The open rooftop terrace catches natural light well into the evening, making it a favorite for golden-hour portraits.

Picture-Perfect Corners Inside VanThara

Beyond the entrance and staircase, the marble coffee table with its decorative globe, the bronze Ganesha idol in the lobby, and the lit indoor water fountain each add a distinct, detailed backdrop for close-up and candid shots.

Space for Your Full Wedding Party

A pre-wedding shoot or saree function often brings more than just the couple — VanThara's 3 floors give hair and makeup teams, family members, and the photography crew separate space to prepare without crowding the shoot itself.

Getting to VanThara for Your Shoot Day

The villa is located in Turkayamjal, close to Ramoji Film City — itself a well-known base for photography and film production in Hyderabad — with access via the Outer Ring Road (ORR).
