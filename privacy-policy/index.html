Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy applies to VanThara Homestay (vantharahomestay.com) and to the social media posting tool we use to publish our own photos and videos to our own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

The tool signs in only to accounts owned by VanThara Homestay. It uploads our own villa photos and videos to our own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It does not read, collect, store or share any information about other people or about viewers of our content.

Our tool uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channel. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to handle your booking or where the law requires it.
