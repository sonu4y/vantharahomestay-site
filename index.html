About the Villa

Your Home Away
from Home

VanThara Homestay is a grand, fully independent 5-bedroom villa spread across three spacious floors. Designed for large families and group gatherings, it blends modern luxury with a warm, homely atmosphere.

From the ornate gold-accented entrance gate to the elegant curved staircase, every corner of this property tells a story of meticulous craftsmanship. Relax in the home theatre, cook in the professional kitchen, work out in your private gym, or unwind on the open rooftop terrace.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can VanThara Homestay accommodate?

The villa has 5 bedrooms across 3 floors and comfortably hosts 15+ guests, making it ideal for large families, reunions and team offsites.

Do we get the entire villa to ourselves?

Yes — always. VanThara is booked as one entire private villa. We never split it between different groups.

How is pricing decided?

Rates depend on your dates, group size and occasion. Send your dates through the booking form or WhatsApp and the host will share a custom quote — introductory rates are currently available for early guests.

Can we host events like birthdays, haldi functions or team offsites?

Yes. The villa regularly hosts birthday celebrations, pre-wedding and saree functions, family reunions and corporate offsites, with a home theatre, rooftop terrace and open living areas to use.

Is parking available?

Yes — covered car parking inside the secure gated compound, with CCTV surveillance.

Where exactly is the villa?

1-22/3, Plot 35, New Kammaguda, Torrur Road, Turkayamjal, near St. Paul's School, Hyderabad – 501510. It is close to Ramoji Film City and reachable via the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and LB Nagar.

What amenities are included?

A 75-inch 4K home theatre, fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, private gym, open rooftop terrace, AC in all 5 bedrooms, high-speed WiFi, RO purified water, 24/7 power backup and hot water.

How do I book?

Check the availability calendar, send the booking form, or message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 77357 26603. The host replies personally to every enquiry.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp — a real person answers.
